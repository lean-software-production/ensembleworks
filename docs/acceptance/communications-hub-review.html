01 · The feature

A shared conversation, available to each thread.

We already had a scribe turning the V1 room’s audio into text. This work makes that text available through BB’s existing conversation tools, alongside sources such as Zoom.

FOLLOW

Read speech as it arrives

An active conversation opens at the latest speech and updates automatically. There is no need to keep clicking “Load more.”

UNDERSTAND

See readable speaker blocks

Nearby chunks from one speaker join into readable blocks, including across brief interjections. Individual passages keep their references.

USE

Give a thread conversation context

Attach a conversation to a BB thread, then ask it to find, explain or summarise relevant discussion with citations back to what was said.

02 · How it works

From spoken discussion to usable context.

Team speaksThe existing V1 Canvas voice room carries the discussion.
→
Scribe transcribesThe existing local service produces speaker-labelled text.
→
Hub receivesThe adapter checks for new speech every few seconds, stores it and avoids duplicate passages on retries.
→
People & threads readThe same conversation supports a live view, search and stable citations.
Capture and reading are independent. Capture continues when a viewer pauses or a thread detaches. Each attached thread has its own reading position. Speech is reference material: receiving it does not automatically start agent work or make spoken instructions authoritative.

03 · Team acceptance walkthrough

Try it with a real conversation.

  1. Open the conversation.In Communications Hub, choose “EnsembleWorks V1 team transcript.” This thread’s initial setup included the preceding hour, then followed new speech.
  2. Speak a short contribution.Watch it arrive without loading another page. Check whether the speaker labels and grouping make it easy to read.
  3. Scroll back or search.The view should hold your place. Choose “Follow live” to return to the latest speech. Pausing the view does not stop capture.
  4. Attach a BB thread.Choose the conversation in the thread’s Conversation panel. Ask: “What options did we discuss, and what remains undecided? Cite the relevant passages.”
  5. Check the evidence.Open a citation and compare the answer with the discussion. Try a second thread: its reading position should be independent.

04 · Acceptance context

Working today, with boundaries to understand.

Evidence from this work

We connected the local V1 scribe, imported recent speech and verified new passages arriving in the Hub. Automated checks covered capture recovery, duplicate handling, thread isolation, live following and grouping.

The user confirmed the grouping fix after an actual speaker’s contribution was split too aggressively. Those passages now join while keeping their individual citations.

Important limits

  • Transcription can be wrong or incomplete. A running connection does not prove every word was captured.
  • Very late speech or a large catch-up gap may be missed or interrupt capture. This is not a guaranteed complete record.
  • Grouping is a reading aid. The 15-second EnsembleWorks window can join distinct remarks or split a long contribution; it does not detect meaning.
  • The live view keeps a recent window. Older stored speech remains available through search and citations.
  • Capture currently has an explicit start and stop, with one active V1 source per Hub. It does not automatically split into meetings or attach every thread.
05 · Decisions for the team

What should we learn or change next?

These are open questions for acceptance review, not a committed roadmap.

Where should a conversation begin and end?

Is one ongoing room transcript useful, or would deliberate sessions or daily conversations be easier to navigate?

How should threads get access?

Is choosing a conversation explicitly the right default? Would a clearer attachment shortcut help people use it?

Does the reading experience feel right?

Try different speakers and natural pauses. Are blocks too large, too fragmented, or hard to inspect while following live?

What confidence and visibility do we need?

How should people see that capture is active, notice a gap, and understand how long their discussion remains available?

Suggested review outcome: agree whether this is useful enough for everyday team use, identify any acceptance blockers, and choose the most valuable next improvement.
